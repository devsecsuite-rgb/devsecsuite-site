Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DevSecSuite collects, uses, and protects information when you use devsecsuite.com.

The Short Version

Your tool inputs never leave your browser. When you decode a JWT, generate a UUID, hash text, or use any other tool, all processing happens locally on your device. We never see, store, or transmit your input data.

What We Collect

We use standard web analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns: browser type, device type, pages visited, country or region, and referring URL. We do not collect the content of anything you type or paste into our tools.

Cookies and Advertising

We use cookies for analytics and advertising. Third parties we work with include Google Analytics (aggregate traffic analysis) and Google AdSense (display advertising). Google uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.

Client-Side Processing

All tools on this site process data in your browser using JavaScript. No input is sent to any server. You can verify this by opening your browser's developer tools and watching the Network tab while using any tool.

Your Rights

You can request information about any personal data we hold by emailing hello@devsecsuite.com. Since we don't require accounts or collect tool inputs, we typically hold almost no personal data about you.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted on this page with a revised date.
