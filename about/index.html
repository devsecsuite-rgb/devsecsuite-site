About DevSecSuite

DevSecSuite is a collection of free, privacy-first tools for developers and security engineers.

Why We Built This

Every developer has a bookmark folder full of sketchy tools that either require signup, send your data to an unknown server, or are cluttered with popups. We built DevSecSuite to be the opposite: fast, private, and free.

Privacy First

Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent to our servers. No accounts. No tracking of your inputs. When you decode a JWT, generate a UUID, or hash a password, it happens entirely on your device using client-side JavaScript.

How We Make Money

DevSecSuite is supported by display advertising. We work with privacy-respecting ad networks and never sell user data. Ads are the only reason these tools stay free and unrestricted.

Open Web Standards

We build against open standards — RFC 4122 for UUIDs, RFC 7519 for JWTs, standard crypto APIs for hashing. No proprietary formats, no vendor lock-in.
