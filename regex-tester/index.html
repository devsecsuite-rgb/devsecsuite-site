Regex Syntax Cheatsheet

Character Classes

. any char   \d digit   \w word char   \s whitespace   [abc] any of a,b,c   [^abc] none of a,b,c

Quantifiers

* 0+   + 1+   ? 0 or 1   {n} exactly n   {n,m} n to m

Anchors

^ start   $ end   \b word boundary

Groups

(abc) capture group   (?:abc) non-capture   | alternation

Frequently Asked Questions

Which regex flavor is this?

JavaScript (ECMAScript). It's close to most other flavors but has some differences from PCRE, Python, and Java. Lookaheads work; lookbehinds work in modern browsers.

What does the "g" flag do?

Global — find all matches, not just the first. Without g, only the first match is returned.

Do you log my regex or text?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers.
