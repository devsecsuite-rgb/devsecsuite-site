Fast, private, browser-based utilities for developers and security engineers. Everything runs locally — no data is ever sent to a server.

Why DevSecSuite?

Most online tools send your data to a server. Ours don't. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your JWTs, hashes, and certificates never leave your machine.

Built for developers who need a quick answer without a signup, an install, or a privacy trade-off.
